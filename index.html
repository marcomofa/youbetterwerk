Recovery. Stability. A Path Back to Work.

You Better Werk is an employment program for people in recovery who are ready to take the next step toward work. We believe that meaningful employment can support stability, confidence, and long-term recovery.

Who It’s For

You Better Werk supports individuals with substance use histories who are interested in working or returning to work—especially if employment has been interrupted and you want flexible, individualized support.

How It Works

This program is built around real-world employment and individualized support. Instead of long pre-employment training, we help participants connect to work sooner—with coaching while they’re working.

Dedicated case manager

Personalized support based on your goals and readiness.

Job placement specialist

Support identifying opportunities and connecting with employers.

Goal setting & planning

One-on-one planning to match work with your real-life priorities.

Ongoing coaching

Support while you’re working to address barriers and sustain employment.
