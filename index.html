Recovery. Stability. A Path Back to Work.

What to Expect

You Better Werk is an employment program for people in recovery who are ready to take the next step toward work. We believe meaningful employment can support stability, confidence, and long-term recovery.

This program focuses on real-world employment support, not long training periods before anything happens. Participants receive individualized guidance and coaching while working.

Your first step is an information session at Stepping Stone. It’s a low-pressure way to learn about the program, ask questions, and decide if it feels like the right fit.

You don’t need a résumé. You don’t need to have everything figured out. You just need interest in taking a step toward work.

If you’re interested after the info session, the next step is a one-on-one meeting with a case manager to explore goals, readiness, and interest in joining the program.

Who It’s For

You Better Werk is designed for individuals with substance use histories who are:

Curious about working again

Exploring what’s possible—at a pace that fits you.

Returning after an interruption

Getting support rebuilding your path back to work.

Not sure where to start

Identifying strengths, goals, and next steps with support.

Looking for ongoing coaching

Receiving support before, during, and after employment.

Not sure if it’s the right fit? We’re here to talk through your questions and help you explore what works best for you.

About Our Partners

About Options For All

Options For All (OFA) is a California 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to creating and supporting opportunities for adults with intellectual and/or developmental disabilities (I/DD). We exist to create and support opportunities for adults with I/DD in making choices to live, work, and love life in their community with dignity and respect. Our team creates hope, inspires dreams, and enables our clients to live a life without limits—Options For All: the best place to be.

About Stepping Stone

Stepping Stone aims to help end the stigma surrounding recovery from alcohol and substance use and to remind people that help is available and no one has to deal with this issue alone. What happens here ripples out and changes the lives of partners, spouses, parents, siblings, and friends—creating a community that goes far beyond groups and meetings. We are Stepping Stone—volunteers, staff, residents, outpatient clients, donors, supporters, family members, friends, neighbors, and more. I am Stepping Stone. You are Stepping Stone.
