You Better Werk

Recovery. Stability. A path back to work — with individualized support while you build momentum.

What to Expect

You Better Werk is an employment program for people in recovery who are ready to take the next step toward work. We believe meaningful employment can support stability, confidence, and long-term recovery.

This program focuses on real-world employment. We support participants with individualized coaching while they’re working, not months of training before anything happens.

Your first step is a low-pressure information session at Stepping Stone, where you can learn more, ask questions, and meet the team.

You don’t need a résumé. You don’t need to have everything figured out. You just need interest in taking a step toward work.

Participation requires eligibility through the California Department of Rehabilitation (DOR). We can help you start that process.

Is This Program Right for You?

You Better Werk may be a good fit if you are:

💪Curious about working again

Exploring what’s possible — at a pace that fits you.

🔄Returning after a break

Looking for support rebuilding your path back to work.

🧭Not sure where to start

Want help identifying strengths, goals, and next steps.

🤝Seeking ongoing support

Interested in coaching before, during, and after employment.

Not sure if it’s the right fit? We’ll talk through your questions and help you explore your options.

How It Works

You’ll have a team supporting your goals, readiness, and employment journey.

🧑‍💼Dedicated case manager

Personalized support based on your goals and readiness.

🧩Job placement specialist

Help connecting with employers and opportunities.

📝One-on-one planning

Guidance aligning work with your real-life priorities.

🏁Ongoing coaching

Support addressing barriers and sustaining employment.
