Recovery. Stability. A Path Back to Work.

You Better Werk is an employment program for people in recovery who are ready to take the next step toward work. We believe meaningful employment can support stability, confidence, and long-term recovery.

Who it’s for

Who It’s For

Flexible, individualized support aligned with recovery and readiness.

You Better Werk is designed for individuals with substance use histories who are interested in working or returning to work and benefit from flexible, individualized support aligned with recovery and readiness.

How It Works

Real-world employment support, with personalized coaching while you’re working.

This program focuses on real-world employment—not months of training before anything happens. We support participants with individualized coaching while they’re working.

Dedicated case manager

Personalized support based on your goals and readiness.

Job placement specialist

Help connecting with employers and opportunities.

One-on-one planning

Guidance to align work with your real-life priorities.

Ongoing coaching

Support to address barriers and sustain employment.
